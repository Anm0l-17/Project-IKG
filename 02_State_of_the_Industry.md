# Purpose

Before building a product, we must first understand the ecosystem in which it will operate.

This document analyses the current landscape of news consumption, information retrieval, knowledge management and AI-assisted research.

The objective is not to criticise existing platforms.

The objective is to identify opportunities where India Knowledge Graph (IKG) can provide unique value.

---

# Research Questions

This document attempts to answer the following questions.

• How do people currently consume news?

• Why do users struggle to retain information?

• What problems remain unsolved?

• What solutions already exist?

• Why hasn't anyone solved this completely?

• Where can IKG provide unique value?

---

# The Modern Information Problem

The internet solved access to information.

It did not solve understanding.

Every day, millions of articles are published.

The average reader experiences:

• Duplicate reporting

• Contradictory headlines

• Missing historical context

• Poor follow-up tracking

• Information overload

• Fragmented sources

As a result,

people remember headlines,

not events.

---

# Information vs Knowledge

Information answers

"What happened?"

Knowledge answers

"What happened,

why,

how,

what changed,

and what follows?"

Most existing systems optimise for information.

IKG optimises for knowledge.

# Why Verification Matters

Modern readers consume information from multiple sources.

Verification currently requires manual effort.

Example

Read GKToday

↓

Search The Hindu

↓

Search Indian Express

↓

Compare

↓

Decide

IKG automates this workflow.

---

# Why Timelines Matter

Most important Events evolve over weeks or months.

Example

Bill Introduced

↓

Committee Review

↓

Lok Sabha

↓

Rajya Sabha

↓

President

↓

Gazette Notification

↓

Implementation

Traditional news treats these as independent articles.

IKG models them as one evolving Event.
