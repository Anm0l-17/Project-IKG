# Executive Summary

India Knowledge Graph (IKG) is an AI-powered Event Intelligence Platform designed to transform fragmented news into structured, trustworthy knowledge.

Instead of presenting articles as isolated pieces of information, the platform identifies the underlying real-world Event, verifies it across multiple trusted publications, continuously tracks its evolution, and visualises its relationships with policies, organisations, institutions and historical developments.

The objective is not merely to improve news consumption.

The objective is to improve understanding.

---

# The Problem

The internet has solved information access.

It has not solved information understanding.

Every day, thousands of articles are published across multiple news organisations.

These articles often:

- repeat existing information
- describe only one stage of a larger event
- omit historical context
- fail to explain relationships
- overwhelm readers with duplicates

Readers must manually answer questions such as:

- Is this true?
- Is this related to yesterday's news?
- Has Parliament already passed this?
- Is this a follow-up?
- What happened before this?
- Who benefits?
- Which ministry is responsible?
- Which policy does this affect?

The burden of connecting information remains entirely on the reader.

IKG exists to remove that burden.

---

# Why Existing Solutions Fail

Existing platforms optimise for engagement.

IKG optimises for understanding.

Examples:

Google News aggregates articles.

Inshorts compresses articles.

Traditional news portals publish articles.

Search engines retrieve documents.

None of them attempt to build a structured representation of reality.

The result is an endless stream of information rather than cumulative knowledge.

---

# Vision Statement

Create India's most trusted knowledge platform that enables anyone to understand how events evolve, how they connect, and why they matter.

The platform should become the preferred destination for readers who seek clarity instead of volume.

---

# Mission Statement

Transform verified information into structured knowledge using artificial intelligence, semantic reasoning and knowledge graphs.

Enable every reader to answer:

"What is happening?"

"Why is it happening?"

"What changed?"

"What is connected?"

"What might happen next?"

---

# Product Philosophy

IKG is based on one central belief.

News is evidence.

Events are reality.

Knowledge is the relationship between events.

Therefore,

The platform should never prioritise article quantity.

It should prioritise event quality.

---

# Guiding Principles

## Principle 1

Truth through corroboration.

No single publication should determine the visibility of an Event.

Independent confirmation increases confidence.

---

## Principle 2

Context before chronology.

Readers should understand why something happened before simply knowing when it happened.

---

## Principle 3

Relationships create knowledge.

Understanding emerges from connections rather than isolated facts.

---

## Principle 4

Artificial Intelligence should assist.

It should never become a black box.

Every important AI decision should be explainable.

---

## Principle 5

Trust is earned.

Verification status must always be visible.

The system should explain how verification was reached.

---

## Principle 6

Information evolves.

Events are living objects.

They continue to grow as new developments occur.

---

## Principle 7

Simplicity is intelligence.

A powerful platform should remain approachable.

Complexity belongs inside the system, not on the screen.

# Success Metrics

The project is successful when users can:

Find a verified Event quickly.

Understand an Event without reading five separate articles.

Discover related developments naturally.

Navigate years of history through a graph.

Trust the verification process.

Understand AI reasoning.

The platform should reduce time spent searching while increasing confidence in understanding.

---

# Non-Goals

The platform will NOT attempt to:

Compete with breaking-news publishers.

Replace journalism.

Generate sensational headlines.

Recommend content for engagement.

Optimise for advertisement revenue.

Maximise user screen time.

Encourage addictive consumption.

Become another social media platform.

Publish opinions or editorials.

Predict political outcomes.

These are explicit non-goals.

---

# Design Philosophy

The interface should communicate calmness.

Readers should feel they are exploring a research library rather than scrolling through social media.

Every interaction should encourage exploration rather than distraction.

Animations should communicate relationships.

Colours should communicate meaning.

Typography should communicate hierarchy.

Whitespace should communicate confidence.

Nothing should exist purely for decoration.

---

# Decision Framework

Every future feature request should answer five questions.

1.

Does this improve understanding?

If not,

reject.

---

2.

Does it improve trust?

If not,

reconsider.

---

3.

Can the AI explain its decision?

If not,

redesign.

---

4.

Will this increase long-term maintainability?

If not,

simplify.

---

5.

Would removing this feature make the product worse?

If not,

do not build it.

# Closing Statement

India Knowledge Graph is built on a simple belief:

A nation's understanding should not depend on how many articles someone can read.

It should depend on how well those articles are transformed into connected, verifiable knowledge.

Every engineering decision, every AI model, every database, every interface and every line of code should ultimately serve this purpose.

If a future contributor is ever uncertain about a technical decision, they should return to this document.

The correct decision is the one that best advances the mission of helping people understand India through trustworthy, structured knowledge.
